## Remaining non-technical approvals

The architecture is now explicit, but production activation still requires decisions that engineering cannot safely invent:

- merchant of record;
- invoice/tax issuer rules;
- platform fee/revenue recognition;
- cancellation/refund/chargeback policy;
- vendor payable/payout policy;
- final reconciliation SLA;
- final RTO/RPO/SLO and retention approval;
- provider selection and credentials;
- paid Pre-Need legal/reserve model.

These are gates, not documentation omissions.
