# Post-Burial Experience

## Modules

### Visitation

- visiting hours and instructions;
- optional booking and capacity;
- facilities such as tent/chair requests;
- block/plot navigation according to access policy.

### Grave care

- one-off or recurring work order;
- before/after evidence;
- customer acceptance/complaint;
- vendor replacement and make-good.

### Memorial and QR

- public/private profile;
- approved biography/media;
- QR token resolving to a revocable route;
- family editors and moderation;
- immediate unpublish and abuse reporting.

## Privacy

Grave registry, visitation, and memorial have separate field projections. A public memorial does not make heir contact or private documents public.
