# Requirements — Memorial and QR

**Status:** Optional/privacy-gated P2/B07.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Memorial defaults private and requires authority/consent evidence for editors/publication.
2. Privacy modes: private, family-only, unlisted, public.
3. Public projection uses an explicit field/media allowlist.
4. QR resolves through opaque revocable token and never embeds restricted identifiers.
5. Authorized moderator can immediately unpublish and rotate/revoke token.
6. User-generated messages/media are moderated and reportable.
7. Memorial lifecycle is separate from authoritative grave record.
8. Deletion/retention follows approved policy while audit/evidence is preserved as required.
