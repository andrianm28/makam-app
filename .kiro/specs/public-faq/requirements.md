# Requirements — Public FAQ

**Authority:** Stakeholder Workflow MVP — FAQ.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Public FAQ is reachable from homepage and global navigation.
2. It contains the six required categories from `faq-catalog.md`.
3. Users can filter by category and search title/summary/body.
4. Article detail shows updated date, related articles, and customer-service CTA.
5. Admin can manage draft, preview, publish, unpublish, ordering, and version.
6. Unpublished articles never appear publicly or in search.
7. Payment/Urgent content reflects active gates and approved operational information.
8. Empty search gives related categories and customer-service path.
9. FAQ is responsive, accessible, and indexable where appropriate.
